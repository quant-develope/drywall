Loutraki

Apartments in town blocks, holiday homes, rental rooms and seafront hotels. Much of our work here is renovation before the summer season: partitions to turn a studio into a one-bedroom flat, suspended ceilings with hidden lighting and sound insulation between rooms.

Perachora, Pisia and Lake Vouliagmeni

Detached and holiday houses, often older, with walls that need lining and ceilings under tiled roofs that need insulation. In houses near the sea we pay special attention to damp.

Corinth

Homes, offices and shops in the city of Corinth. Here we often do commercial spaces: shops, clinics and offices that change layout.
