In short

Drywall is a proven material for interiors: light, fast and clean. It needs the right board for each room and a proper frame, and then it lasts as long as a built wall.

Strength and safety

Is drywall strong? A wall with a proper frame and double board stands up to normal household use, wall cabinets with reinforcement and knocks from furniture. For busy spaces there are high-hardness boards.

How does it behave in an earthquake? Because it is light, it loads the building less than a brick wall, and the metal frame absorbs small movements.

Does it burn? Gypsum contains water and slows fire down. Type F fire-resistant boards are used where a study calls for a specific fire rating.

Damp

In wet rooms we use moisture-resistant board and waterproofing under the tiles. On walls with active damp we do not install drywall until the problem is solved. See bathrooms and kitchens.

Timing and organisation

How long does the work take? A partition 1–2 days, a living-room ceiling 2–3 days, then 1–2 days for filling. A whole apartment usually 5–10 working days.

What should I do before you come? Clear the space as far as possible and decide where lights and sockets will go. We take care of the rest, including floor protection and cleaning.

Permits and apartment blocks

Interior layout changes in drywall, without touching the load-bearing structure, usually need a small-scale works approval or no permit at all. Every case is different, so ask your engineer. In an apartment block, let the building manager know the working hours.

Frequently asked questions

How soon can you come for a site visit?

In Loutraki usually within 2–3 working days. If you send photos on WhatsApp first, we come to the appointment with a first idea of the solution and the price.

Do you price per square metre?

Yes, the quote shows a price per m² for each type of work, with extras such as hidden lighting, mineral wool or doors listed separately. The final price depends on height, board type and finish level, so we give it after measuring.

Do you also paint?

We hand over the wall filled and sanded, ready for primer. If you like, we arrange the painting with a partner painter, so you have one person for the whole job.

Do you work in occupied homes?

Yes. We cover floors and furniture, cut boards outside or in one spot as far as possible, and clean up every afternoon. Drywall has none of the mortar and rubble of brickwork.

Do I need a permit for a drywall partition?

Interior layout changes that do not touch the load-bearing structure usually need a small-scale works approval or nothing at all, depending on the case. In an apartment block, let the building manager know. Check with your engineer to be sure.
