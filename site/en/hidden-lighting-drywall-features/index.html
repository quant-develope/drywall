Hidden lighting and drywall features

A TV wall with hidden cables, lit niches beside the bed, an arch in the hallway or an LED channel around the whole ceiling. Small builds that completely change how a room looks.

  • A dimensioned sketch before we start
  • Channels for LED strips and drivers

In short

We first show you a simple dimensioned sketch, so you know exactly where the light falls and where the cables go. Most living-room features are finished in 2–4 days including filling.

TV walls

We build a lining a few centimetres in front of the wall, reinforced where the bracket goes. HDMI, power and network cables run inside and come out right behind the TV. Shelves or lit niches can go on either side.

Hidden lighting in ceilings and walls

The LED strip sits in a channel that hides it from every point in the room. We size the channel so the light spreads evenly, without dots. We suggest warm white 2700–3000 K for living rooms and bedrooms. See also suspended ceilings.

Niches, shelves and arches

Niches beside the bed instead of bedside tables, bookshelves in the hallway, arches in openings and curves in ceilings. For curves we use thin 6.5 mm flexible board.

Frequently asked questions

Who does the electrics?

Your electrician or ours. We leave the channels and openings where we agreed.

Can a drywall shelf take weight?

A drywall shelf holds ornaments and a few books. For heavy loads we put a timber or steel core inside.

Can you build something from a photo I liked?

Yes, send us the photo on WhatsApp and we will tell you whether and how it works in your space.
