Wet rooms

Drywall in bathrooms and kitchens

In a bathroom, drywall hides pipes and concealed cisterns, creates shampoo niches in the shower and straightens walls before tiling. We use only green moisture-resistant board and waterproof before the tiles go on.

  • Moisture-resistant type H board
  • Waterproofing with tape at the shower corners
  • Reinforcement for wall-hung toilets and vanities
Get a quoteWhatsApp

In short

Moisture-resistant board copes with bathroom humidity, but not with running water. That is why in the shower zone we always apply a liquid waterproofing membrane with tape in the corners before tiling, or use cement board for a walk-in shower.

What we build in a bathroom

  • Walls for a concealed cistern and wall-hung toilet, with the steel frame inside.
  • Shower niches, with LED lighting if you want.
  • Boxing to hide vertical soil pipes.
  • A ceiling in moisture board with IP44 or IP65 recessed spots.

In the kitchen

In the kitchen, drywall hides the cooker hood and its duct, closes the space above the cabinets and straightens walls so units fit properly. For wall cabinets we add timber or OSB reinforcement behind the board.

Waterproofing before tiling

Tiles and grout are not waterproof. Water slowly gets through and, if it reaches plain drywall, the board swells. So the order is always: moisture board, primer, two coats of waterproofing with tape in the corners, then tiles. We work with your tiler or suggest one of ours.

From the crewIf the bathroom has no window, it needs an extractor fan. We plan it in the ceiling with a duct to the outside.

Frequently asked questions

Is green drywall waterproof?

No. It is moisture-resistant, meaning it copes with humid air. In the shower it needs waterproofing on top before tiling.

Can I tile on drywall?

Yes, with C2 tile adhesive. For large, heavy tiles we space the studs closer and use double board.

How long does it take?

For a 5–6 m² bathroom, the drywall work is done in 2–3 days, before the tiler.
