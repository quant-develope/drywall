Prices

What drives the cost of drywall

Drywall is usually priced per square metre, but two square metres do not always cost the same. Here we explain what raises the price and what you can do to keep it down without spoiling the result.

Get a quoteWhatsApp

In short

The biggest factors are the design (flat, or with steps and curves), the number of boards per side, insulation and the finish level. Our written quote lists every item separately, so you see what you are paying for.

What raises the price

FactorWhy it matters
DesignA flat suspended ceiling is the cheapest. Steps, LED channels, curves and niches need more framing and much more labour.
Board typeMoisture-resistant, fire-resistant and acoustic boards cost more than standard board.
LayersDouble board per side means twice the material and more screws, but much better sound insulation and strength.
InsulationMineral wool is added per m², depending on thickness and density.
HeightAbove 3 metres you need scaffolding and more time.
FinishLevel Q2 is enough for matt paint. Q4, with filler over the whole surface, is needed for grazing light and glossy paints.
AccessA third floor with no lift or a narrow stairwell add time for carrying materials.

How to keep the cost down

  • Group all drywall work into one phase, so the crew does not come back several times.
  • Decide in advance where lights, sockets and cabinets go, to avoid cutting openings later.
  • Keep hidden lighting where it will really be seen, for example in the living room.
  • Ask for Q2 where there is no grazing light.

Frequently asked questions

Why don't you publish a price per m²?

Because the same ceiling can cost very differently depending on design, height and finish. A price without measuring would be misleading. An indicative price from photos is free.

Is the site visit charged?

No, the site visit and written quote are free in Loutraki, Corinth and Perachora.

How is payment made?

Usually a deposit for materials and the balance on completion. For large projects, by stage.
