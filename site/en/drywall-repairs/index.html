Small jobs

Drywall repairs

A hole from a door handle, a joint that opened in the ceiling, a section that swelled after a leak. Small drywall damage is fixed quickly and disappears after painting, as long as it is done properly.

  • Replacing swollen sections after a leak
  • Cracked joints re-taped
  • Access hatches where missing
Get a quoteWhatsApp

In short

Small repairs usually take two visits: on day one we cut out, fit a new piece and apply the first filler, and on day two we finish. If the damage is from water, we first make sure the leak has stopped.

On this page

  1. Holes and breakage
  2. Cracked joints
  3. Water damage

Holes and breakage

We cut the damaged part into a clean rectangle, add a backing support, screw in a new piece of the same thickness and apply tape and filler. After sanding and painting, the repair cannot be seen.

Cracked joints

If joints keep opening, tape is usually missing or the frame moves. We open the joint, check the screws and hangers, and redo it with fibreglass or paper tape.

Water damage

Drywall that got wet swells and softens. It does not dry back properly, so we replace it. We also check the insulation behind, which often needs replacing too. If needed, we coordinate with your plumber.

Frequently asked questions

Do you come for small jobs?

Yes. We often group them with other work in the same area so we can come sooner.

Will the repair show?

After sanding and painting the whole wall, no. If only the patch is painted, the colour may differ slightly.

Do you repair other crews' drywall?

Yes, we look at what is wrong and tell you whether a repair is enough or a section needs redoing.
