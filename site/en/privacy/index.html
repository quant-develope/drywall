What we collect

When you fill in the form, we receive by email your name, phone, area, type of work and message, and the page you sent it from. If you write to us on WhatsApp, Viber or by email, we have whatever you sent there.

Only to reply to you, arrange a site visit and send you a quote. We do not pass it to third parties and do not send marketing messages.
