Heat, cold, noise

Thermal and sound insulation with drywall

Noise from the neighbours, cold from the north wall and summer heat under the roof can often be solved from inside, with a drywall frame full of mineral wool. No scaffolding, and the outside of the building does not change.

  • Stone wool or glass wool inside the frame
  • Acoustic boards and resilient profiles for noise
  • Vapour barrier where needed

In short

For noise from the next apartment, the most effective solution is an independent lining with mineral wool and double acoustic board that does not touch the old wall. For cold or heat, insulation goes on the outside walls or on the ceiling under the roof.

Sound insulation: what really helps

Sound passes through the wall and through every gap. Good sound insulation has three parts: mass (double or acoustic board), absorption (mineral wool inside the frame) and decoupling (the new wall does not touch the old one, and resilient tape goes at the edges).

Egg-box foam and thin polystyrene do not insulate against sound. They absorb a little echo inside the room, but do not stop noise from next door.

Thermal insulation from inside

When external insulation is not possible, for example in an apartment block, insulating from inside is the practical answer. With 5–8 cm of stone wool and drywall, the wall stops feeling cold to the touch and the air conditioner works less.

On ceilings under a tiled roof or in attics, insulation above the suspended ceiling makes the biggest difference in summer.

Damp and vapour barriers

With insulation on the inside, warm indoor air must not reach the cold wall, or it condenses. Where needed we add a vapour-barrier membrane or boards with a built-in barrier. In Loutraki, with the humidity from the sea, this matters.

Sound insulation in hotels and holiday lets

Guest reviews often mention noise from the next room. A sound-insulating lining on the party wall takes a few days per room, out of season. See the hotels page.

Frequently asked questions

How much noise will it cut?

It depends on the existing wall and on the gaps. A proper independent lining with double board and mineral wool makes normal conversation next door inaudible. We do not promise total silence from bass-heavy music.

Stone wool or glass wool?

Both insulate well. Stone wool is denser, insulates sound better and resists fire longer. Glass wool is lighter and cheaper.

Can it be done only on the ceiling?

Yes. For footsteps from the flat above we fit a suspended ceiling on resilient hangers with mineral wool above.
