Commercial spaces

Drywall for hotels and shops

Loutraki lives on tourism, and every winter hotels and rental rooms are renovated to be ready before Easter. We work to a schedule per floor or per room, so the business knows in advance when each space closes and reopens.

  • Suspended ceilings with hatches for AC and fire detection
  • Fire-rated partitions and linings in corridors
  • Sound insulation between rooms

In short

For hotels we visit every room, quote per room type and give a schedule per floor. Where fire protection is required, we use certified manufacturer systems as specified in your engineer's fire-safety study.

Hotels and rental rooms

The most common jobs are new ceilings in rooms and corridors, sound insulation on party walls, boxing for new pipework and linings in bathrooms before new tiles. We work from November to March, and in operating hotels if needed, keeping noise down during quiet hours.

Shops, restaurants and offices

In a shop on the seafront or in the main market, time is money. Dry construction lets the layout change in a few days: new fitting rooms, counters, a lit ceiling, partitions for a storeroom or office.

Fire protection

Stairwells, escape corridors and plant rooms often require a fire rating such as EI 60 or EI 90. We achieve it with type F fire-resistant boards and a system tested by the manufacturer, built exactly as described in the system sheet.

From the crewFire protection is specified by an engineer's study. We build the system the study calls for and keep photos of every stage for the file.

Scheduling and invoicing

For commercial spaces we give a price per square metre or per room, a schedule, and an invoice. We coordinate with your engineer, electrician and HVAC technician so each trade knows when it comes in.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work while the hotel is open?

Yes, in sections that close temporarily, and we schedule noisy work at times that do not disturb guests.

Do you issue invoices?

Yes, for all work.

How early should we book for the winter?

The earlier the better. The schedule fills up in autumn, so for January–March work it is best to talk by October.
