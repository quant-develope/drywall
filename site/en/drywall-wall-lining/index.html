Walls

Drywall wall lining

An old house in Loutraki or Perachora often has crooked walls, plaster that bubbles and cracks that come back after every repaint. Drywall lining gives a new, straight surface in front of the old wall, without tearing anything out.

  • On a frame or bonded, depending on the wall
  • With thermal insulation on outside walls
  • Cables and sockets run inside
Get a quoteWhatsApp

In short

If the wall is dry and fairly straight, we bond the board directly with plaster adhesive and you lose only 2 cm. If it is crooked or you want insulation, we build a frame and you lose 5–8 cm.

On this page

  1. Bonded lining or on a frame
  2. Thermal insulation from inside
  3. When lining is not the answer

Bonded lining or on a frame

Bonded lining uses dabs of plaster adhesive, directly on the plaster or brick. It is quick and cheap, but needs a dry wall without large hollows.

Framed lining is built on CD or CW profiles a little in front of the wall. It corrects any unevenness, leaves room for cables and insulation, and is not affected by the old plaster.

Thermal insulation from inside

On outside walls, especially north-facing ones, lining is a chance to add insulation. We fill the frame with stone wool or bond ready-made insulated plasterboard with expanded polystyrene. More on the insulation page.

When lining is not the answer

If the wall has active damp from rising water or a leak, lining will hide the problem and go mouldy behind. In that case the cause is fixed first and the drywall goes on afterwards.

From the crewIn ground floors near the sea we often see damp at the bottom of walls. We measure it with a moisture meter at the site visit before suggesting a solution.

Frequently asked questions

How much space do I lose?

About 2 cm with bonded lining, 5–8 cm with a frame, and a little more with thick insulation.

Do I need to strip the old plaster?

No, unless it is falling off. With a frame the board does not touch the old plaster at all.

Can you line just one wall of a room?

Yes. We often line only the outside wall or only the TV wall.
