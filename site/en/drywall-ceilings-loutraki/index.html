Ceilings

Drywall suspended ceilings in Loutraki

A suspended ceiling hides cables and pipes, levels a crooked or cracked ceiling and gives you a place for spotlights, LED strips and air-conditioning vents. We hang it from the slab on adjustable hangers, as low as it needs to be.

  • From 5–6 cm below the slab for simple levelling
  • Steps, recesses and perimeter hidden lighting
  • Access hatches for AC units and manifolds

In short

For a 25–30 m² living room, the ceiling is framed and boarded in 2–3 days and filled in another 1–2. The price depends mostly on the design: a flat ceiling costs much less than one with steps and hidden lighting. See what drives the cost.

How a suspended ceiling is built

First we mark the final height on every wall with a laser and fix the UD perimeter track. From the slab we hang rods every 80–100 cm and attach the CD 60×27 main channels to them. The frame is levelled, and only then do we screw on the boards across the channels, staggering the joints so they never line up.

The joints get tape and filler in two or three coats. Corners get corner beads so they stay straight and do not chip.

Hidden lighting and spotlights

Hidden (cove) lighting is an LED strip inside a channel along the perimeter or around a recess. The light falls on the ceiling and the room feels taller. We design the channel together with the electrician, so the driver fits and the strip is easy to replace.

Recessed spots need at least 8–10 cm of space below the slab. If you want recessed spots across the whole living room, tell us at the site visit, because that sets how far the ceiling drops. More on the hidden lighting page.

Air conditioning, ventilation and hatches

In new homes and in Loutraki's hotels, AC ducts or ducted indoor units often go inside the ceiling. We leave openings for the vents and hinged access hatches, so filters can be cleaned and the technician can reach the unit without breaking anything.

Ceilings in bathrooms, balconies or under a roof

In bathrooms we use green moisture-resistant board, and the room must be ventilated. Under a roof or in an attic we first add insulation and a vapour barrier. For outdoor areas such as balcony soffits there are cement boards that withstand rain and sea salt. Ask us about your case.

From the crewIf the slab shows damp or flaking plaster, we tell you before we start. A ceiling hides the problem, it does not solve it. Waterproofing first, drywall second.

Frequently asked questions

How much lower will the ceiling be?

5–6 cm for simple levelling, 8–12 cm if you want recessed spots, and more if AC ducts run through. On a 2.70 m ceiling a simple suspended ceiling does not change the feel of the room.

Will the ceiling crack over time?

Not if the frame is hung correctly and the joints are taped. Cracks usually come from too few hangers or filler without tape, and we do neither.

Can I hang a light fitting?

Yes. For heavy fittings we add a reinforcement in the frame beforehand, or fix directly to the slab. Tell us in advance where they will go.
